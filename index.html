I want to build a fence for my backyard. What materials do I need?

For a basic wood backyard fence, you’ll typically need:

  • Fence posts: pressure-treated 4x4s
  • Concrete: to secure the posts
  • Rails: horizontal 2x4s
  • Fence pickets/boards: cedar or pressure-treated wood
  • Exterior screws or galvanized nails
  • Gate materials: boards, hinges, latch
  • Gravel: for drainage beneath posts
  • Wood stain / sealer: optional, but helps longevity

FAQ

Ask us.

Tap a question. We answer the way we would on the phone.

How does an ad show up inside ChatGPT?

Assistants now show sponsored cards under their answers, matched to what the person is actually asking for rather than a keyword. We set up the account, targeting, creative and reporting. Same job a search agency does, on a newer surface.

Is this just SEO?

No. SEO ranks a page. This is paid placement inside the answer, plus making sure the facts about your business (service area, hours, services, reviews) are consistent enough that an assistant is willing to name you at all.

What do you need from me?

Your service area, the jobs you want more of, your hours, a budget, and a phone number that gets answered. First campaign is usually live within a week.

How do I know it’s working?

Impressions, clicks and calls per platform, every month, in plain language. We also re-run your category through the assistants so you can see the answer change. No long-term contract.

Is Google AI Mode live in Canada?

Ads inside it aren’t fully rolled out here yet. We set up your feeds and business data now, so you’re live the day it opens instead of six weeks behind.
